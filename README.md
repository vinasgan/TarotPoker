# 🃏 Tarot Poker: Project Overview
Tarot Poker is a tactical card game that merges the deep strategy of Poker with the mystical and unpredictable nature of a 78-card Tarot deck. 

## 🎴 The Deck
Unlike a standard 52-card deck, this game utilizes the full 78-card Tarot structure:

56 Minor Arcana (The Suits): These are your standard poker cards. There are 4 suits (Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles). Each suit has 14 cards.

22 Major Arcana (The Events): These cards do not have suits. They act as powerful "Event Cards" that manipulate the rules of the game.

### The 14-Card Suit Hierarchy

2 through 10: Standard numerical values.

11: Page (Jack equivalent).

12: Knight (The unique Tarot rank).

13: Queen.

14: King.

15: Ace.

## 🎮 Game Rules & Mechanics
### The objective is simple: Be the first to win 3 rounds.

### 1. Setup: The Fate Dealing
At the very start of the game, 3 Event Cards are dealt to each player.

These cards are assigned for the entire duration of the match.

## 🎮 The Round Structure
Each round is a tactical progression of card dealing and decision-making.



### 1. Start of round: The Deal & Replenishment
First Round: Each player starts with their initial hand of Event Cards.

Subsequent Rounds: At the start of every new round (excluding the first), each player is dealt one additional Event Card.

Hole Cards: Each player receives 2 private Minor Arcana cards.

### 2. The Community Table & Event Windows
The round progresses as cards are dealt to the center of the table.

After each deal, players enter an Event Window:

3 Cards on Table: The first Event Window opens.

4th Card on Table: The second Event Window opens.

5th Card on Table: The third and final Event Window opens.

### 3. Window Rules & The Pass Mechanic
In each Event Window, players have exactly one opportunity to act:

Trigger an Event: Activate one Major Arcana card to shift the game's fate.

Pass: do not use the card.

Round Cap: Regardless of how many cards you hold, you may only trigger a maximum of 2 Event Cards per round. Once both players pass or use their move, the game moves to the next dealing phase.

### 4. The End of round
Once the final Event Window is closed, the round ends.

Evaluation: Players form the best 5-card hand using their 2 private cards and the 5 community cards.

Victory: The player with the superior Poker combination wins the round.
