Game Rules

Objective

Win 3 rounds to win the match.

Each Round

Each player receives 2 hole cards. The community starts with 3 cards (Flop), then gains a 4th (Turn) and 5th (River). The best poker hand using your 2 hole cards + community cards wins.

Event Windows

There are 3 Event Windows per round — after the Flop, Turn, and River. Both players simultaneously choose to play a Major Arcana card or Pass. You may play at most 2 event cards per round.
